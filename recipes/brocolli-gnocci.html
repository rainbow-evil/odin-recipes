Don't be afraid of brocolli. This is a delicious, cheesy dish. Low in effort, high in yummines. Cheese lovers can add parmesan to cheese it up, while a green salad served along the dish cuts through the heaviness andadds refreshing notes to the course.

  • Prep time: Not a lot
  • Cook time: 15-20 min

Ingredients

  • 500g gnocci
  • 1 brocolli
  • 200 ml heavy cream
  • 100 ml milk
  • 150g shredded cheese (gouda, mozarella, etc)
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 spoons of olive oil
  • salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Wash and cut brocolli. Boil it for 3-4 minutes in salted water, then drain it.
  2. Briefly boil gnocci according to the packaging instructions.
  3. Heat up the olive oil. Add garlic. After a while, mix in heavy cream and milk. Cook for 2-3 minutes until it binds.
  4. Preheat the oven to 200C. Put in a large container gnocci and the sauce. Mix well, then cover with cheese.
  5. Cook for 15-20 minutes, until the cheese melts and gnocci turn golden. Serve with a salad.
