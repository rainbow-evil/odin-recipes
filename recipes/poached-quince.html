Description

Poached quince is a delicious and simple recipe for when you want a pure quince desert without a lot of efford.

  • Prep time: 15 min
  • Cook time: 1 to 2 hours

Ingredients

  • 900g water
  • 225g sugar
  • 1 vanilla bean, split lenghtwise with seeds scraped
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 lemons
  • 4 medium quinces

Instructions

  1. Combine water, sugar, vanilla bean and seeds, and cinnamon in a medium saucepan.
  2. Bring it to a simmer over medium heat until the sugar dissolves and the syrup begins to bubble.
  3. Fill a medium bowl with cold water and add lemon juice.
  4. Peel and quarter the quinces. Drop them into lemon water (to stop them from oxidizing).
  5. Drain the quince pieces out of the lemon water.
  6. Place the quince into sugar syrup and leave on low simmer for an hour or two, until fork tender and rose in color.
  7. Let the quince cool completely in the poaching liquid.
  8. Remove the core and enjoy the dish.
